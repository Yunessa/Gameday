Where the scores come from, and what this thing is built out of.

Live data

Scores, schedules and TV listings come from ESPN's public scoreboard endpoints (site.api.espn.com), requested directly by your own phone or laptop while the page is open. Live games refresh about every 25 seconds; everything else refreshes every few minutes.

This is an unofficial, undocumented ESPN endpoint, not a paid or licensed feed. Scores can lag a few seconds behind the broadcast, go blank during ESPN maintenance, or change format without warning. Never settle a bet with it.

Typography

No web fonts are downloaded. The page uses your device's own system typeface: San Francisco on iPhone, iPad and Mac, Roboto on Android, Segoe UI on Windows. Scores and clocks use the matching system monospace face so the digits stay aligned as they change.

That keeps the page fast on bar wifi and makes it read like the rest of your phone.

Color

A neutral grey-and-white system with a green accent in the neighborhood of Cheba Hut's brand green, tuned for contrast rather than exact brand matching. Red is reserved for live games only. Dark mode follows your phone's setting automatically.

Icons

Every icon is a native emoji drawn by your own device. No icon font, no image library, no third-party icon service is contacted. Team crests are loaded from ESPN's CDN, with a colored initials badge as a fallback when a crest is missing.

Privacy & code

One static HTML file. No build step, no framework, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking of any kind.

Your pinned teams are saved in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device. The only requests this page makes are the score requests to ESPN described above.
